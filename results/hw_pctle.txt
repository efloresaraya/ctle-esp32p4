KEY FINDING
===========
P-CTLE eliminates float multiply (FMUL.S) from the inner loop on RISC-V.
Replacing  acc += LUT_w[nibble] * x[c]      (FMUL.S + FADD.S)
with       acc += LUT_p[(a_idx<<4)|nibble]  (integer addr + FADD.S)
saves ~4 cycles per multiply on ESP32-P4 RISC-V.

Overhead: 256 FMUL.S per GEMV call to build LUT_p (amortised over M*N ops).
  For embedding (32000x288 = 9.2M ops): 256 / 9,216,000 = 0.003% overhead.
  For projection (288x288 = 82,944 ops): 256 / 82,944 = 0.3% overhead.

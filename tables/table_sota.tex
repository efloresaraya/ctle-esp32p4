\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}
  \footnotesize
  \textbf{Why WT2 PPL differs across blocks.}
  GPTQ/SqueezeLLM/LLM.int8() evaluate on models trained on general text
  (Wikipedia, CommonCrawl), so their WikiText-2 PPL is low (5–31).
  TinyStories-15M was trained exclusively on simple children's stories;
  its FP32 WikiText-2 PPL of 6{,}127 reflects out-of-distribution evaluation,
  not a deficiency of the compression method.
  Within our block, the PPL ordering across compression methods
  is valid and consistent with the TinyStories results.

  \textbf{Flash-stream}: inference reads nibbles and LUT/scale directly from Flash
  without reconstructing dense weight matrices in RAM
  (LUT~=~64\,B; only activations and KV cache are allocated in PSRAM).
  GPU methods require full VRAM residency.
  CTLE runs on ESP32-P4 Nano (RISC-V HP\,@360\,MHz, 32\,MB PSRAM, 16\,MB Flash).
